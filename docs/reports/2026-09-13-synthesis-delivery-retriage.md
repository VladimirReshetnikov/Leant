### Reduction hypothesis to test next

A concrete witness uses the function-only carrier `R = (a -> a) -> a -> a` for both supplied-default `foldl1` and `foldr1`. This still establishes no synthesis acceptance or general behavioral proof.**

The carrier returns a function that takes two further arguments, the initial endomorphism and the default. The trace must therefore inspect elimination after the fold as well as carrier selection; the carrier being present alone is insufficient. This is an investigation target, not an established cause.

With typed witnesses now available, trace one original reduction query end to end: can its instantiation plan name the carrier, does search construct the fold step, and do reconstruction and replay preserve it? A bounded closure of function carriers is a possible repair only if the trace establishes that missing carrier as the obstruction. If the carrier is already available, diagnose the missing term derivation instead. Scalar comparator/projection extrema may reuse reduction support; paired extrema and trees may need additional constructions and retain separate acceptance gates.

The bounded experiment has reached its stopping condition: both numeric providers were admitted and `length` still missed. Retain the result and move to evidence/accumulator work unless a trace identifies a specific additional repair.
