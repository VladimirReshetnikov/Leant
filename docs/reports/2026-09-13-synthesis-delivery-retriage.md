### Reduction construction boundary

A concrete witness uses the function-only carrier `R = (a -> a) -> a -> a` for both supplied-default `foldl1` and `foldr1`. It fits Lean's `Type` elimination slot and does not require placing a polymorphic Church list into that slot. A finite Python model passed 18,744 observations, including empty lists, varied defaults and nonassociative operators. **Both witnesses now compile in Haskell and Lean; Lean checks four closed examples and reports no axioms for either fold. This still establishes no synthesis acceptance or general behavioral proof.**

The carrier returns a function that takes two further arguments, the initial endomorphism and the default. The next trace must inspect elimination after the fold and construction of its arguments. The unsuccessful carrier experiment shows why carrier enumeration alone is insufficient evidence of reachability; it does not establish the remaining cause.

The first original-query trace and carrier-only experiment are complete without acceptance. For the next reduction increment, inspect term construction and plan admission: can its instantiation plan name the carrier, does search construct the fold step, and do reconstruction and replay preserve it? Do not expand the carrier vocabulary again without a derivation showing why the retained proposal cannot participate in a required term. The fixed-carrier timeout is diagnostic evidence, not proof that all term-construction routes fail. Scalar comparator/projection extrema may reuse reduction support; paired extrema and trees may need additional constructions and retain separate acceptance gates.

The bounded experiment has reached its stopping condition: both numeric providers were admitted and `length` still missed. Retain the result and move to evidence/accumulator work unless a trace identifies a specific additional repair. Provider-first scheduling must also be checked for starving a useful structural or smaller-provider search; merely moving the starvation elsewhere is insufficient.
