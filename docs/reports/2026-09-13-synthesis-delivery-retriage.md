## What the constructor result changes

The failed constructor baseline spent all 32 raw slots but left 19,140 of its 20,000 choice allowance unused. Duplicate comparison discarded 28 proofs, leaving four implementations. Merely changing normal-form priority also failed. The accepted change instead constructs a use of an existing contextual helper within the normal-term grammar, while retaining the original first proof, original search and shared accounting.

This promotes **construction that incorporates an existing proof-use requirement** over another general scheduling experiment. It does not establish a reduction fix. In Djinn, carrier-focused plans already check that a selected instantiation bridge occurs after a raw proof is generated. The specific next hypothesis is that expressing this existing requirement during construction could reach useful terms earlier. Check plan applicability and exact assumption identity first; preserve independent proof checking, lexical scope, ordinary alternatives, and charging for every raw proof and choice.

The [reduction boundary report](2026-09-13-reduction-carrier-boundary.md) provides a compiler-checked witness using `R = (a -> a) -> a -> a`. Adding bounded continuation carriers did not synthesize the required behavior; a separate fixed-carrier control also timed out. Those observations do not establish which remaining construction transition fails. The experiment remains archived and unreleased. Do not reinstate it unchanged or infer synthesis acceptance from the existence of its witness.

Likewise, the [provider-admission experiment](2026-09-13-behavioral-provider-admission.md) admitted both numeric providers but still missed `length`. Another provider-order change needs a trace showing the useful derivation it recovers without starving structural search.
